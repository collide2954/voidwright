# Body

This document gives the reference values for the body of the wanderer. The values come from measurements of adult men. The reference figure is 1.76 m tall, and its hips are 0.88 m above the ground. The men of the ANSUR II survey have a mean stature of 1.756 m, so the game uses their values almost without change.

The game keeps the legs of the motion reference in motion.md: the hips are at 0.88 m, and the knees are at 0.44 m. Thus the legs are shorter than the mean, and the trunk is longer.

The skeleton has no toe joint, so the boots are shorter than a real foot. A long rigid foot goes into the ground when the heel lifts.

## Athletic build

The wanderer has an athletic build. For the shoulders, the chest, and the muscles of the arms, the neck, and the calves, the game uses the 75th percentile of the men in ANSUR II. For the waist, the game uses the 25th percentile. Thus the chest is wider and deeper than the waist.

The coat cloth stays 7.5 cm from the axis of each thigh. Thus the thighs of the wanderer are thinner than the thighs of real men. The coat hides this.
