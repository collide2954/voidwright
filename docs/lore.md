# Lore

This document gives the facts of the Voidwright world. Text in the game can use a richer style.

## The Gyre

- The Gyre is a ring structure around the star Vael.
- The Wrights built the Gyre. The Wrights were a human civilization of master builders.
- Vael is an amber giant star. Vael is near the end of its life, and it becomes larger each age.
- The Shroud is a nebula around Vael and the Gyre. No ship from outside the Shroud has come to the Gyre.

## History

| Era | Events |
| --- | --- |
| The Founding | The Wrights build the Gyre around Vael. |
| The Sundering | Vael starts to die. To save Vael, the Wrights drive the heart of the Gyre into the void. They want power from the Undertow. A rupture opens. In one night, the Gyre breaks into fragments, and the Wrights go missing. |
| The Drift | The present era. It started at the Sundering, approximately 900 years ago. The survivors build towns on the fragments. Some Wright technology continues to operate. |

## The void

- The void is vacuum with strange physics. It has exotic fields and exotic particles. It does not have a mind.
- The Undertow is the exotic field that the Wrights used. Near deep pockets, the Undertow pulls on matter.
- Where the Undertow touches matter for a long time, magenta crystals grow.
- Tech attacks get their power from the Undertow. The Resonance skill tunes the body to the rhythm of the Undertow.

## Peoples

| People | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Clans | Descendants of the survivors. Each clan has a home region, a culture, and a style of dress. |
| Constructs | Wright repair machines that grew minds of their own. |
| The changed | Humans that long exposure to the Undertow changed. |

## Clans

Each player comes from one clan. A player leaves home to become a voidwright. "Voidwright" is a title for a person who masters the crafts of the Wrights.

| Clan | Home | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mossfolk | Moss plains | Farmers and herders. They are patient, they like festivals, and they do not trust the deep. |
| Divers | Wreck fields | Salvage divers. They are bold and superstitious, and they laugh easily. |
| Cutters | Glass forests | Crystal miners. They are quiet and precise. The Undertow changed many of them partially. |
| Spanners | The Spans | Bridge builders and traders. They talk much and bargain hard. |
| Driftkin | Raft fleets | Nomads who sail debris rafts between the fragments. They are restless and generous, and they are the best pilots on the Gyre. |
| Lamplighters | Hull wastes | Keepers of the old Wright beacons on the outer hull. They are solemn, and they listen to the rhythm of the Undertow. |
| Tinkers | Machine cathedrals | Families who live with constructs and repair them. Some of them think of machines as family. |
| Netcasters | Edge harbors | Void trawlers who fish the drift from skiffs at the edges of the fragments. They are patient storytellers. |
| Kilnfolk | Sunward cliffs | Smiths who forge in the heat of Vael. They are proud, loud, and scarred. |
| Hearthkeepers | Garden domes | Cooks and brewers. They operate the galley halls, where all clans meet in peace. |
| Trappers | Nightward reaches | Hunters who track void animals in the dark. |
| Keepers | Drowned decks | Archivists who guard the Wright records and argue about their meaning. |

## Places

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Moss plains | Hills of teal moss under amber light, with farms, herds, and clan towns. |
| Glass forests | Groves of magenta crystal. The crystal hums when a field storm passes. |
| Drowned decks | Flooded Wright habitat levels. The water hangs in strange gravity. |
| Machine cathedrals | Large Wright halls that continue to operate. Constructs guard them. |
| Wreck fields | A maze of ship hulls that drift together. |

| Undertow scars | Places where the Undertow pulls: bent gravity and pockets of slow time. They are the most dangerous places on the Gyre. |

| Garden domes | Wright gardens under broken domes. Plants grow over everything. |
| Hull wastes | Outer hull plates without air. Players must wear suits there. |
| Dry docks | Large Wright shipyards with arks that are not complete. |

## Powers

| Power | Description |
| --- | --- |
| The Steward | The control mind of the Gyre. It is broken, but it is awake. It speaks in fragments through old terminals. |
| Wardens | The Wardens think that the Steward wants repair. They want to repair the Gyre and save Vael. |
| Reclaimers | The Reclaimers think that the Steward is a lock. They want to take the Gyre apart and leave before Vael dies. |
| Ascendants | The Ascendants think that the Steward is a guide. They want to change into a form that does not need a star. |

## Threats

| Threat | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Hollow swarms | Wright repair machines that operate incorrectly. They eat structure to make copies of themselves. |
| Leviathans | Very large animals that feed on the Undertow. They drift between the fragments. |
| Raiders | Human crews that attack traders and towns. |
| Field storms | Storms of exotic particles. They move across fragments, change the terrain, and hurt people without protection. |

## Story

- The tone is wonder with dark depths. Towns have warmth and humor. Ruins cause awe. Deep wrecks cause dread. A quest can be funny or sad.
- Three mysteries move the long story: Where did the Wrights go? Can Vael be saved? What is outside the Shroud?
- The quest line of each legion tells a different version of the truth. Each version is partially true. A player learns the full truth only from all three versions.

## Hidden truths

Do not show these facts to players before the story shows them.

- The Wrights did not die. The rupture pulled the heart of the Gyre into a pocket of slow time. For the Wrights in the heart, the Sundering occurred some minutes ago. They can come back.
- The Wrights made the Shroud. The Shroud hides Vael from something outside. The Sundering made the Shroud weaker, and it continues to become weaker. This is part of the endgame.
